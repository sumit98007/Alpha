Alpha also sends placeholder-integrity metadata: its prompt, chat, or guidance source, a request ID, and occurrence count. When local protection is enabled and detection succeeds, detected categories, detected values, and the local restoration map stay on this device. Recognised sensitive-value patterns are redacted locally when protection is enabled, but automated detection can miss sensitive data. This protection applies only to Alpha’s refinement path; the host AI page controls its own composer and may already observe or process drafts you type.

Alpha has not read or transmitted the selected prompt text or chat. It will do so only after you continue. The host AI page remains separate and controls its composer.
